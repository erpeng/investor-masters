# Data Checklist

Use current data for any real-time bottom judgment. Always cite date and source. If a number cannot be verified, mark it as unavailable instead of guessing.

## Evidence Grades

| Grade | Sources | Use |
|---|---|---|
| A | exchange filings, company announcements, regulators, official fund/ETF issuer pages, CFTC, central banks | core facts and tables |
| B | market data vendors, broker pages, financial portals, official quote pages | price, volume, valuation, short selling, holdings snapshots |
| C | media, channel checks, dealer quotes, resale platforms, social/search data | weak evidence for narrative and channel temperature |

## Minimum Data for a Concrete Report

Price and valuation:
- Latest close, intraday range if relevant, 52-week/cycle high and low.
- Drawdown from high and distance from low.
- PE/PB/dividend yield or other relevant valuation anchor if meaningful.
- Market cap and currency.

Volume and price action:
- Biggest down-volume days in the current decline.
- Retests of the low and their volume versus 20/60/65-day average.
- Whether bad news still creates new lows.

Holder and flow pressure:
- Fund/ETF holdings, shares, redemptions, creations, or AUM flows.
- Northbound/southbound, HKSCC/CCASS, shareholder count, top holders.
- Insider/substantial shareholder changes.

Leverage and short pressure:
- Short selling ratio and short turnover.
- Margin financing/securities lending.
- Futures open interest and positioning for futures-driven assets.
- Options skew or borrow pressure if material and available.

Fundamental/channel pressure:
- Revenue, profit, margin, cash flow.
- Inventory, inventory turnover days, receivables, contract liabilities.
- Product/channel prices or asset-specific proxy from `asset-maps.md`.
- Management guidance and quarterly business updates.

Narrative:
- What the old bullish narrative was.
- What the current bearish narrative is.
- Whether new bad news has less price impact than before.

## Quality Rules

- Separate official facts from interpretation.
- Use absolute dates; avoid "recently" without a date.
- Do not turn media channel checks into hard accounting facts.
- Explain proxy substitutions. Example: for Pop Mart, Labubu resale premium is a batch-price substitute, not reported revenue.
- If a key indicator is missing, include it in the follow-up tracking checklist.
