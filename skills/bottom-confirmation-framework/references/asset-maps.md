# Asset Indicator Maps

Use this file to choose the right bottom-confirmation indicators for the asset. Keep the universal framework, but replace proxies by asset type.

## Universal Layers

| Layer | Question | Common indicators |
|---|---|---|
| Price pressure | Has price already repriced the bad news? | drawdown from 52-week or cycle high, distance from low, valuation reset, support zone |
| Panic selling | Did forced/emotional selling cluster? | volume spike, turnover spike, gap down, long lower shadow, price no longer making new lows on bad news |
| Institutional/passive pressure | Are large holders still reducing? | fund holdings, ETF shares, index inclusion/exclusion, northbound/southbound, CCASS/HKSCC, shareholder count |
| Leverage/short pressure | Are leveraged or short positions still pressing? | short ratio, securities lending, margin balance, futures open interest, CFTC COT, options skew |
| Fundamental/channel pressure | Is the real-world driver still worsening? | inventory, channel price, contract liabilities, order backlog, product resale premium, utilization, commodity inventory |
| Narrative pressure | Has the story shifted from denial to disgust? | headlines, sell-side language, social/search heat, management commentary, repeated bad-news sensitivity |
| Confirmation | Are bad news and selling being absorbed? | lower volume retests, support holds, high-volume reversal, fundamentals stop worsening |

## Gold / Commodities

Primary indicators:
- Spot/futures/ETF price drawdown and support zones.
- ETF holdings, tonnes, weekly flows, and issuer shares outstanding.
- CFTC COT: Managed Money longs, shorts, net long, open interest.
- Dollar index, real rates, VIX, inflation expectations, central bank demand.
- Futures curve, inventories, lease rates where relevant.

Bottom confirmation:
- ETF outflows slow or stop.
- CFTC speculative net long falls from crowded levels.
- Open interest falls during decline, then stabilizes without price breaking lower.
- Bad macro headlines stop producing new lows.

## A-Share Companies

Primary indicators:
- Price drawdown, PE/PB/dividend yield, volume and turnover.
- Shareholder count, top-10 holders, public fund holdings, ETF holdings.
- Northbound/HKSCC where relevant.
- Margin financing balance and securities lending.
- Latest annual/quarterly reports: revenue, profit, cash flow, inventory, receivables, contract liabilities.
- Industry high-frequency data: prices, orders, utilization, channel inventory.

Bottom confirmation:
- Shareholder count stops rising or starts falling.
- Public funds/northbound stop persistent reduction.
- Earnings revisions stop worsening.
- Business high-frequency data stabilizes before the income statement fully recovers.

## Hong Kong Stocks

Primary indicators:
- Price drawdown, valuation reset, volume versus 20/60/65-day average.
- Southbound holdings, CCASS participant changes, substantial shareholder disclosures.
- Short selling shares, short turnover, short ratio.
- Options/derivatives availability and borrow/hedging pressure when material.
- Company filings and business updates.

Bottom confirmation:
- Southbound holdings stabilize for multiple weeks.
- Short ratio falls or high shorting no longer makes new lows.
- Substantial shareholders or insiders buy near the support zone.
- Bad news is absorbed without breaking the prior low.

## Liquor / Baijiu

Primary indicators:
- Core product batch price and terminal price, such as Feitian Moutai, Gu20, Gu16.
- Channel inventory, dealer willingness to prepay, contract liabilities.
- Wholesale versus direct-sales mix, dealer count, cash collections.
- Public fund holdings, northbound/HKSCC, shareholder count.
- Revenue/profit decline narrowing, gross margin, selling expense, inventory.

Bottom confirmation:
- Core batch price stops making new lows for several weeks.
- Contract liabilities and cash collections improve together.
- Channel price stabilizes without heavy subsidies.
- Fund/northbound reduction slows and price holds on weak channel news.

## Consumer / IP / Product-Cycle Companies

Primary indicators:
- Price drawdown, valuation reset, volume, southbound/northbound or fund holdings.
- Product resale premium, second-hand market prices, sell-through, search/social heat.
- New product launch speed, stock-outs versus discounting, user reviews.
- Inventory, inventory turnover days, receivables, store expansion, regional growth.
- Concentration by product/IP/region and evidence of second engine growth.

Bottom confirmation:
- Resale prices stop collapsing and normal products do not broadly break issue price.
- Inventory growth slows or turnover days stabilize.
- Non-core products/IPs start absorbing growth pressure.
- Short/holding pressure fades while price holds the prior low.

## ETFs / Funds / Sectors

Primary indicators:
- ETF shares outstanding, premium/discount, AUM split into flow versus price effect.
- Daily/weekly net creations/redemptions.
- Component concentration and largest holdings drawdowns.
- Sector earnings revisions, fund manager positioning, style factor pressure.

Bottom confirmation:
- Redemptions slow or reverse.
- ETF price stops falling despite outflows.
- Largest components stop making synchronized new lows.
- Sector narrative shifts from denial to disgust while price stabilizes.
